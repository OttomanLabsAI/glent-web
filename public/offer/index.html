Tale of the tape

glent.com todayThe new site
The browser title on the homepage and on every project page is just "Glent" — no page descriptions anywhere for search to show. A full title and description naming the trade, the sectors and the territory — the first thing search shows.
The business is described four different ways across the site; one leftover description still says "commercial, residential and datacentre". One positioning line — self-delivered CSA, Data Centres & Pharma, Europe — used once, everywhere.
The proof is buried: no client names, project count or contract values on the homepage. The €43m flagship sits beside the headline; the facts strip and the full fifteen-project register follow it.
Contact page errors: the office icons are broken images, Denmark is listed with a +44 London number, and Göteborg is misspelt. Offices rebuilt cleanly — the doubtful number carries a visible "to confirm" flag instead of being repeated as fact.
The flagship €43m case study misspells "a basement" as "abasement". Copy proofed end to end.
The page opens on an autoplaying video and the tagline "Building Our Digital Future" before saying what Glent do. The first line states the business; nothing loads before it.
