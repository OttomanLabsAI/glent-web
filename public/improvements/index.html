Tale of the tape

glent.com todayThe new site
Every page's browser title is just "Glent" — nothing for search to show. Full titles and descriptions — the first thing search shows.
The business is described four different ways; one leftover still says "commercial, residential". One line — self-delivered CSA, Data Centres & Pharma, Europe — everywhere.
The proof is buried — no clients, counts or values on the homepage. The €43m flagship beside the headline; fifteen projects behind it, each opening to its sheet.
Contact errors: broken icons, Denmark on a +44 London number, Göteborg misspelt. Offices rebuilt — the doubtful number flagged "to confirm", not repeated.
The flagship case study misspells "a basement" as "abasement". Copy proofed end to end.
Opens on an autoplaying video and a slogan before saying what Glent do. The first line states the business.
