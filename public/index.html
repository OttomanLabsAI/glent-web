The packages

What we self-deliver

Four packages, one contractor — engineered, set out and built by Glent teams with Glent plant.

PKG 01 — Engineering Services

The package starts on the model, not the ground — planning, co-ordination and technical submittals carried from Stage 4 to Stage 5.

  • Preconstruction modelling and planning
  • Logistic plans
  • Stage 4 to Stage 5 technical submittals
  • Underground services co-ordination
  • BIM modelling
  • Temporary works designs
  • Waterproofing detailing

PKG 02 — Groundworks & Underground Services

From bulk dig to drainage — the below-ground package delivered as one scope.

  • Bulk excavation
  • Shoring systems
  • Contaminated ground disposal
  • Contaminated ground remediation
  • Enabling works
  • Pile mat installation
  • Drainage installation
  • Attenuation tanks
  • Pre-cast manholes
  • Service duct installation
  • Pre-cast culvert installation
  • Ground water management
  • Ground beam construction
  • Sheet piling

PKG 03 — Reinforced Concrete

The frame, from raft to core — in-situ, jumpform and pre-cast.

  • Waterproofing
  • Pile caps and crane bases
  • Raft slabs
  • Concrete lift pits
  • Liner walls
  • RC walls and columns
  • RC cores
  • Jumpform cores
  • Suspended slabs
  • Pre-cast concrete stairs
  • In-situ stairs

PKG 04 — Hard Landscaping

Everything outside the slab edge — roads, yards, security lines and street works.

  • Asphalt roads
  • Kerbing
  • Paving
  • RC yard slabs
  • Street works
  • Signage
  • Street furniture
  • Temporary fencing
  • High security fencing & gates
  • Hoarding

How we deliver

One contractor, own plant, audited systems

Delivery

Self-delivery

Glent Group self-deliver the CSA package — engineering, groundworks, concrete and externals under one roof, with the majority of growth coming through repeat business and continuing relationships.

Plant

In-house plant

Glent Plant Ltd, the group's own plant company based in Emmeloord, The Netherlands, keeps machines and programme in the same hands.

HSEQ

Audited systems

An NQA-certified integrated management system — ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 — with a stated goal of industry-leading health and safety performance and an injury-free workplace.

Health · Safety · Environment · Quality

Working to the highest standards

The primary goal is construction-industry-leading occupational health and safety performance and wellbeing — which ultimately means an injury-free workplace.

Glent Group operates and maintains accreditation to an NQA-certified Integrated Management System. ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 share the same high-level structure, so one set of manuals and procedures carries all three — with document and records control, internal audits, and corrective and preventive action required throughout.

Intended outcomes of the system

  • A stronger knowledge base and a proactive approach to a healthy, safe, clean workplace
  • Customer satisfaction and employee pride
  • Continual improvement of the system's performance
  • A culture that supports the system's intended outcomes
  • Worker participation through consultation
  • The resources to keep work safe, healthy and sustainable
  • Policies compatible with the group's strategic direction
  • Hazards identified, risks controlled, opportunities taken
  • Performance monitored through internal audit
  • The system integrated into the group's business processes
  • Impacts to the environment and community minimised
