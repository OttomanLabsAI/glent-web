The packages

What we self-deliver

Four packages, one contractor — engineered, set out and built by Glent teams with Glent plant.

PKG 01 — Engineering Services

The package starts on the model, not the ground — planning, co-ordination and technical submittals carried from Stage 4 to Stage 5.

  • Preconstruction modelling and planning
  • Logistic plans
  • Stage 4 to Stage 5 technical submittals
  • Underground services co-ordination
  • BIM modelling
  • Temporary works designs
  • Waterproofing detailing

PKG 02 — Groundworks & Underground Services

From bulk dig to drainage — the below-ground package delivered as one scope.

  • Bulk excavation
  • Shoring systems
  • Contaminated ground disposal
  • Contaminated ground remediation
  • Enabling works
  • Pile mat installation
  • Drainage installation
  • Attenuation tanks
  • Pre-cast manholes
  • Service duct installation
  • Pre-cast culvert installation
  • Ground water management
  • Ground beam construction
  • Sheet piling

PKG 03 — Reinforced Concrete

The frame, from raft to core — in-situ, jumpform and pre-cast.

  • Waterproofing
  • Pile caps and crane bases
  • Raft slabs
  • Concrete lift pits
  • Liner walls
  • RC walls and columns
  • RC cores
  • Jumpform cores
  • Suspended slabs
  • Pre-cast concrete stairs
  • In-situ stairs

PKG 04 — Hard Landscaping

Everything outside the slab edge — roads, yards, security lines and street works.

  • Asphalt roads
  • Kerbing
  • Paving
  • RC yard slabs
  • Street works
  • Signage
  • Street furniture
  • Temporary fencing
  • High security fencing & gates
  • Hoarding

How we deliver

One contractor, own plant, audited systems

Delivery

Self-delivery

Glent Group self-deliver the CSA package — engineering, groundworks, concrete and externals under one roof, with the majority of growth coming through repeat business and continuing relationships.

Plant

In-house plant

Glent Plant Ltd, the group's own plant company based in Emmeloord, The Netherlands, keeps machines and programme in the same hands.

HSEQ

Audited systems

Management systems aligned to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001 — with a stated goal of industry-leading health and safety performance and an injury-free workplace.
